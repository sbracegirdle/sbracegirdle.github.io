# Where the dates come from

The organiser's own calendar, in every case. These are the pages the current
version of `static/sports.html` was compiled from. Check the date, the venue and
the status on the source before you put any of it on the page — an aggregator
that agrees with a stale calendar is two wrong sources, not confirmation.

Two kinds of link appear below. The **standing** ones are governing bodies and
race organisers, and they survive a season. The **per-edition** ones are hosts —
a Worlds gets a new site every year — and they're worth re-finding rather than
guessing at by editing the year in the URL.

## Road cycling

- Tour de France — https://www.letour.fr/en/ (standing)
- Tour de France Femmes — https://www.letourfemmes.fr/en/ (standing)
- Giro d'Italia — https://www.giroditalia.it/en/ (standing)
- Vuelta a España — https://www.lavuelta.es/en/ (standing)
- Il Lombardia and the other Monuments — https://www.ilombardia.it/en/ and the
  RCS / Flanders Classics organiser sites (standing)
- UCI, for the calendar and the Worlds — https://www.uci.org/calendar
- The Road and Gravel World Championships get a host site per edition.

Route details land months before the race and move: a Grand Tour's stage list is
firm once presented, but individual finish towns have changed. The UCI's own
calendar sets the Worlds programme — time trials, then road races.

## Triathlon

- World Triathlon, for the championship series and the finals —
  https://triathlon.org/ and https://events.triathlon.org/
- IRONMAN, for the World Championships and the regional races —
  https://www.ironman.com/
- T100 — https://t100triathlon.com/

IRONMAN moves its world championship formats around — one field per venue, both
fields on one day, Kona and Nice swapping years. Read the current arrangement
off IRONMAN's own page rather than assuming last year's holds.

## Marathon majors

- Abbott World Marathon Majors — https://www.worldmarathonmajors.com/
- The individual races publish their own dates: Tokyo, Boston, London, Sydney
  (https://www.tcssydneymarathon.com/), Berlin
  (https://www.bmw-berlin-marathon.com/en/), Chicago
  (https://www.chicagomarathon.com/), New York
  (https://www.nyrr.org/tcsnycmarathon)

The list of majors is not fixed. Sydney was added; Cape Town and Shanghai have
been through the candidate process. Check how many there are before writing a
number into the section intro.

## Formula 1

- https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/ — the season calendar, with round numbers
- Individual promoters, for weekend detail and new circuits

Round numbers on the page (`R11 ·`) come from that calendar and shift if a round
is cancelled or added mid-season. The FIA sets sprint weekends, and they change
year to year, so don't carry a "gets a sprint" note across seasons.

## Rugby

- All Blacks — https://www.allblacks.com/
- Wallabies — https://wallabies.rugby/
- World Rugby, for the Nations Championship and tour windows —
  https://www.world.rugby/

Test venues are announced late and change often; the page notes them as
unconfirmed rather than guessing. Which competitions run in a given year is not
stable either — the Rugby Championship makes way for tours in some years.

## Cricket

- Cricket Australia — https://www.cricket.com.au/
- New Zealand Cricket — https://www.nzc.nz/
- Western Australia — https://www.wacricket.com.au/
- Perth Scorchers — https://www.perthscorchers.com.au/
- The host board for away series (e.g. https://www.cricket.co.za/)
- ICC, for the Future Tours Programme — https://www.icc-cricket.com/

The FTP gives series months out; boards confirm grounds and start dates later. A
multi-format tour (Tests, ODIs and T20Is) is one fixture row on this page unless
the formats are far enough apart to sit in different months.

## What tends to be wrong a month later

- A venue listed as "to be confirmed" — check these first on any refresh
- Any fixture whose date is a range ending in a month name rather than a day
- Sprint weekends, round numbers, and anything counted ("2 Tests", "5 ODIs")
- A championship's format, which changes more often than its date
