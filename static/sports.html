what's left of the season

Six sports, and the people and teams I follow in them: Grand Tours, world championships, every Grand Prix, the Bledisloe, and whatever Australia, New Zealand, WA and the Scorchers are playing.

Snapshot

Compiled 25 July 2026 from official calendars. Sports calendars move, so check the organiser's page before you plan around one. The names under each event are the ones I follow, not a confirmed start list.

Season at a glance

October is the heaviest month: a Monument, two world championships, three Grands Prix, both Bledisloe Tests, and the Sheffield Shield and WBBL both starting.
